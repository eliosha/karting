// DRIVER SAFETY CODE

SAFETY IS OUR #1 PRIORITY

All racers undergo a brief marshall safety induction before hitting the track.

👟

Closed Shoes Mandatory

Sneakers or trainers required. No high heels, crocs, or open sandals allowed on track.

🪖

Helmets & Hairnets

Full-face sanitized helmets and hygienic hairnets provided complimentary for every racer.

🚩

Marshall Flag Control

Certified marshalls oversee every lap. Green (race), Yellow (hazard), Red (stop), Checkered (finish).

🔞

Age & Height Rules

Cadets (ages 6-12), Pro Karts (ages 13+), Two-Seater passengers (ages 5+ with adult driver 18+).
