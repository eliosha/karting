EVENT PROGRESSION

Grand Prix Format

01
Paddock Sign-In & Briefing

Helmet fitting, kart seat adjustment, and safety track rules briefing by race marshals.

02
Practice & Qualifying Heats

Dial in your braking points, master the racing line, and set your best lap for grid placement.

03
Standing Grid Final Race

Line up in qualifying order. Watch the 5 red starting lights — when lights go out, the sprint begins.

04
Podium & Trophy Ceremony

Top 3 podium celebration, celebratory photo shoots, and post-race crew refreshments.

MARSHAL SYSTEM

Trackside Flags

Green Flag · Track Clear

Circuit is all clear. Green light is on, full racing throttle permitted.

Yellow Flag · Caution Ahead

Hazard or spin ahead. Reduce speed immediately — strict no overtaking rule.

Red Flag · Session Suspended

Stop racing safely, slow right down and return under control to the pit lane.

Chequered Flag · Finish Line

Session completed. Finish your final lap at reduced speed and enter pit lane.

DRIVER READINESS

Track Essentials

  • ✓
    Closed-Toe Shoes Required

    Sneakers or trainers are mandatory for pedal grip. No sandals or open shoes on track.

  • ✓
    Sanitized Helmet & Hairnet

    Provided on-site for every racer. Hair must be neatly tucked before buckling up.

  • ✓
    Arrive 15–20 Mins Early

    Ensures ample time for registration, seat fitting, and driver briefing without rush.

  • ✓
    Zero Alcohol Before Driving

    Safety is non-negotiable. Drivers must be sober when entering the karting cockpit.
